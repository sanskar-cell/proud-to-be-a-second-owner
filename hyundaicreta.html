          Hyundai Creta Specifications

          The Hyundai Creta has 1 Diesel Engine and 2 Petrol Engine on offer. The Diesel engine is 1493 cc while the Petrol engine is 1497 cc and 1482 cc . It is available with Manual & Automatic transmission.Depending upon the variant and fuel type the Creta has a mileage of 17.4 to 21.8 kmpl. The Creta is a 5 seater 4 cylinder car and has length of 4330 mm, width of 1790 mm and a wheelbase of 2610 mm.
