          BMW 3 Series

          4.3102 ReviewsRate & Win ₹1000

          The BMW 3 Series has been one of the most desirable luxury sedans in India for decades with its combination of performance for enthusiasts and practicality for the family. We get it in two iterations: long-wheel base for the standard diesel and petrol versions as well as the sportier M340i. It is a typical sedan with low-slung styling, comfortable and spacious rear seats, and a big boot, but also has punchy performance for those who like to drive and be driven in.

          BMW 3 Series price

          3 Series has 1 variants — M340i xDrive (₹77.10L) — the performance flagship of BMW's iconic sports sedan, powered by the 3.0L TwinPower Turbo inline-six (374 PS / 500 Nm) with 8-speed Steptronic Sport and xDrive all-wheel drive — 0-100 km/h in 4.4 seconds, making it the quickest car BMW builds in India. Standard kit covers adaptive M suspension, M Sport differential, variable sport steering, M Sport brakes, 37.97cm Control Display + 31.24cm digital cluster, Harman Kardon 16-speaker surround sound, electric glass sunroof, adaptive LED headlamps with cornering light, Vernasca leather sport seats with memory, 3-zone climate, parking assistant with reversing assistant, wireless CarPlay + Android Auto and 19-inch M light alloys. No trim to pick: at ₹77.10L you get the six-cylinder sports sedan that enthusiasts buy as the last of its kind — a daily driver that embarrasses supercars at traffic lights.
